## Название (до 30 символов)
Kyrgyzstan Wallpapers & Cards

## Краткое описание (до 80 символов)
HD wallpapers of Kyrgyzstan and greeting cards for Eid, Nooruz and Fridays

## Полное описание (до 4000 символов)
Kyrgyzstan on your phone: the Tian Shan mountains, Issyk-Kul lake, yurts, mosques and national ornaments — plus beautiful greeting cards to send to family and friends on WhatsApp.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• New wallpapers and cards are added every day
• The feed gathers pictures from all categories and shows them in a new order every time you open it
• Fresh pictures appear at the top of the feed right away

GREETING CARDS FOR WHATSAPP
• Ready-made cards for Nooruz, Orozo Ait (Eid al-Fitr), Kurman Ait (Eid al-Adha), New Year, March 8, Victory Day and Independence Day
• Friday (Juma), good morning, good night and birthday cards in Kyrgyz and Russian
• Write a name or your own greeting right on the card and choose the text color and size
• Send to WhatsApp with one tap
• A reminder the day before a holiday that the cards are ready

HIGH-QUALITY WALLPAPERS
• Categories: nature, animals, architecture, religion, people
• Set as home screen, lock screen or both
• Auto-change: a new wallpaper every day from your favorites or the whole collection
• Save to your gallery and share with friends

EASY TO USE
• Favorites: save the pictures you like with a tap on the heart
• Search by words: "mountains", "mosque", "Nooruz"
• Swipe down to close a picture, just like in social apps
• Light and dark theme, or follow the system
• Three languages: Kyrgyz, Russian and English

Photos from Wikimedia Commons are published with their author and license.
